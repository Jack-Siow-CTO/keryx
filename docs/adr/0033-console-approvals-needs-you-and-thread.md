# Approvals use Needs you list attention and sticky in-thread cards

Status: **accepted** (grill 2026-07-28).

Pending Approvals (and similar Inbox projection items) are always discoverable from a **Needs you** system row in the chat list, and when the related Session is open also as a **sticky action card** above the composer. Opening Needs you navigates into context (source Session or focused Approval surface)—not a peer permanent rail, not list-only hunting, and not full-app modal interrupt as the only pattern. Chosen so high-blast decisions stay unmissable under a messaging IA without restoring dual-rail cockpit navigation.
